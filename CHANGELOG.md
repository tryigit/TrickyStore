# Changelog

## V2.7.5

- Improved device verification and keybox compatibility across supported devices.
- Added clearer StrongBox, TEE, and RKP information in the Keybox Hub.
- Made custom keybox selection and certificate handling more consistent.

- Strengthened reliability during startup, app restarts, and background activity.
- Reduced delays in common key and certificate actions.
- Improved WebUI clarity and language support.
- Updated core components for broader compatibility and stability.
